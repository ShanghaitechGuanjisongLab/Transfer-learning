摘  要
在自然环境中，动物需要不断利用既有经验应对新的任务和情境。已有行为学研究表明，既往经验可促进后续学习——即使新旧任务的感觉线索来自不同模态。然而，这种跨模态的快速连续学习在大脑皮层中如何实现仍不清楚。
行为学观测表明，连续学习的优势建立在两个方面：一是显著提高了动物对新任务的早期行为表现，二是显著加快了对新任务的学习速率。
利用在体双光子钙成像技术和化学遗传学的干预手段，本研究对这两种行为学优势的神经机制分别进行了探讨。针对早期表现的提高，研究发现新任务的线索刺激优先重新激活了旧任务中的记忆印迹细胞；而抑制这些旧任务中活跃的细胞，或延长新旧任务的时间间隔以降低重激活比例，均会显著削弱新任务早期的行为表现。这表明，连续学习中被重激活的皮层神经元通过稳定皮层响应，支持了新任务的早期表现。
针对后续学习速率的加快，研究发现连续学习中小鼠的运动皮层神经元保持了由旧任务塑造的、较高分化状态的活动特征。分析表明，这种响应的分化有利于旧任务信息在新任务中的复用与重组。对不同脑区的抑制实验发现，丘脑的活动强度维持了连续学习过程中运动皮层神经元的响应分化特征；抑制丘脑活动会降低皮层群体响应分化程度，并显著减慢新任务的学习进程。最后，本研究通过构建稀疏循环神经网络连续学习模型进行数值模拟，发现连续学习加强了兴奋性与抑制性突触的差异，导致神经元响应异质性增加。该模型从机制上提出了连续学习加速新任务学习的可能性，验证了细胞群体的异质性响应能有效提高学习信号的信噪比，从而加快参数更新，并阐释了丘脑输入减弱对学习速率的负面影响。
关键词：连续学习，细胞重激活，皮层响应分化，丘脑调控，初级运动皮层 
Abstract
In natural environments, animals constantly rely on prior experience to adapt to novel tasks and contexts. Behavioral studies have shown that past experience can facilitate subsequent learning, even when the sensory cues across consecutive tasks differ in modality. Yet the cortical mechanisms that enable this rapid, cross-modal continual learning remain poorly understood.
Our behavioral analyses reveal that the benefits of continual learning can be dissociated into two independent components: a markedly higher initial performance when first exposed to the new task, and a significantly faster rate of subsequent learning.
Using in vivo two-photon calcium imaging and chemogenetic manipulations, we investigated the neural mechanisms underlying each component. For the elevated initial performance, we found that the novel cue preferentially reactivated memory engram cells formed during the prior task. Chemogenetically inhibiting these cells, or prolonging the inter-task interval to naturally degrade their reactivation, substantially impaired early performance on the new task. These results indicate that reactivated cortical neurons support early behavioral execution by stabilizing population-level responses.
For the accelerated learning rate, we discovered that motor cortical neurons in mice undergoing continual learning retained a highly differentiated response profile shaped by the prior task. This response differentiation facilitated the efficient reuse and reorganization of information from the earlier task. Through region-specific inhibition experiments, we identified thalamic input as essential for maintaining this cortical response differentiation: suppressing thalamic activity reduced population-level heterogeneity and markedly slowed the acquisition of the new task. Finally, we constructed a sparse recurrent neural network model to simulate continual learning. The model showed that prior training amplifies the heterogeneity between excitatory and inhibitory synaptic weights, thereby increasing neuronal response heterogeneity. It further demonstrated that heterogeneous population responses can enhance the signal-to-noise ratio of learning signals, accelerating parameter updates, and confirmed that weakened thalamic input impairs learning speed — together providing a mechanistic account of how continual learning accelerates novel task acquisition.
Keywords: continual learning, cellular reactivation, cortical response differentiation, thalamic modulation, primary motor cortex
