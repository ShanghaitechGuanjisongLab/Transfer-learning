# Introduction

## 段落 1

迁移学习是认知神经科学研究的重要现象之一，探讨个体如何将已获得的知识、技能和经验应用到新的学习情境中。这一现象广泛存在于动物界，从无脊椎动物的简单行为模式到哺乳动物的复杂认知过程，都存在迁移学习。动物迁移学习的神经机制研究，不仅是揭示大脑学习与记忆基本原理的重要切入点，也能够为人工智能算法的发展提供启示。

> Transfer learning is a central phenomenon in cognitive neuroscience, concerning how previously acquired knowledge, skills, and experience are applied to new learning contexts. This phenomenon is widespread across the animal kingdom, from simple behavioral patterns in invertebrates to complex cognitive processes in mammals. Investigating the neural mechanisms of transfer learning in animals is therefore important not only for understanding the fundamental principles of brain-based learning and memory, but also for informing the development of artificial intelligence algorithms.

## 段落 2

关于动物迁移学习的研究历史悠久（Thorndike, 1898；Thorndike & Woodworth, 1901），但以往的研究多集中于行为学、心理学及脑区尺度成像。对其中神经机制的认识仍较为粗略，尤其缺乏对神经环路和细胞群体动态的直接解析；此外，迁移过程中关键神经元集群、环路重组及动态编码机制也仍有待系统揭示。

> Research on animal transfer learning has a long history (Thorndike, 1898; Thorndike & Woodworth, 1901), but earlier work focused mainly on behavior, psychology, and brain-region-level imaging. As a result, the neural mechanisms underlying transfer remain only coarsely understood. In particular, direct analyses of neural circuits and population dynamics are still limited, and the key neuronal ensembles, circuit reorganization, and dynamic coding mechanisms involved in transfer have yet to be systematically clarified.

## 段落 3

近年来，随着技术手段的进步和理论框架的迭代，对学习记忆神经机制的深入研究已经成为可能。在技术层面，大规模神经记录技术的发展使得记录大规模神经元群体活动成为现实（Chen et al., 2013），并且结合多种神经调控技术后，围绕学习与记忆的研究也迅速增加（Machado et al., 2022）。在理论层面，记忆痕迹细胞理论为学习记忆研究提供了重要框架；该理论从细胞标记、神经调控到环路机制系统阐述了记忆存储与提取中的关键过程（Tonegawa et al., 2015）。本实验室此前利用在体双光子成像追踪即刻早期基因表达，首次在哺乳动物大脑中揭示了新皮层中层特异性、稀疏编码的长时记忆痕迹细胞，为皮层参与学习记忆的神经机制提供了直接的在体证据（Xie et al., 2014）。

> In recent years, advances in experimental techniques and theoretical frameworks have made deeper investigation of the neural mechanisms of learning and memory possible. Technically, the emergence of large-scale neural recording methods has enabled the monitoring of population activity across large numbers of neurons (Chen et al., 2013), and the combination of such approaches with diverse neural manipulation tools has greatly expanded research on learning and memory (Machado et al., 2022). Conceptually, the theory of memory engram cells has provided an important framework for understanding learning and memory by linking cell tagging, neural manipulation, and circuit-level mechanisms involved in memory storage and retrieval (Tonegawa et al., 2015). In previous work from our laboratory, in vivo two-photon imaging of immediate-early-gene expression revealed layer-specific, sparsely coded long-term memory engram cells in the mammalian neocortex, providing direct in vivo evidence that the cortex participates in learning and memory mechanisms (Xie et al., 2014).

## 段落 4

本研究主要采用双光子钙成像技术。该技术具有高空间分辨率、大视野覆盖范围以及能够对同一结构进行长期监测等优势。结合化学遗传学与活动依赖的遗传标记等神经调控技术，可以进一步实现对特定细胞类型或特定活跃细胞群体的标记与操纵。

> The present study mainly employed two-photon calcium imaging. This technique offers high spatial resolution, broad field coverage, and the ability to monitor the same structure over extended periods. When combined with chemogenetics and activity-dependent genetic tagging approaches, it also enables the labeling and manipulation of specific cell types or behaviorally recruited cell populations.

## 段落 5

本研究对头部固定小鼠初级运动皮层（MOp）2/3 层与 5 层进行了跨天双光子成像，并采用“提示刺激-延迟-给水”范式来研究迁移学习的机制性问题。结果表明，跨模态迁移并非单一的整体增强，而是由多层次神经机制共同支撑。首先，预训练中形成的任务相关细胞在新任务开始时发生重激活，并与更高的初始表现相关；与此同时，群体活动的试次间散度降低，提示旧有表征有助于系统更快进入稳定的 learned-like 状态。进一步地，后续学习速度与更高的响应异质性相关，说明迁移还依赖于继承下来的群体分化结构。因果操纵结果显示，丘脑主要影响这种细胞反应分化及其支持的后续学习过程，而 RSP 可能参与将视觉线索信息传递到感觉-运动转换通路中，但并非决定迁移成败的核心要素。

> We performed across-day two-photon imaging in layers 2/3 and 5 of the primary motor cortex (MOp) in head-fixed mice and used a cue-delay-water paradigm to investigate the mechanisms of transfer learning. Our results show that cross-modal transfer is not a unitary global enhancement, but instead is supported by multiple levels of neural mechanisms. First, task-related cells established during pre-training were reactivated at the onset of the new task and were associated with better initial performance. At the same time, trial-to-trial divergence in population activity was reduced, suggesting that prior representations helped the system enter a more stable learned-like state more rapidly. Moreover, the speed of subsequent learning was associated with greater response heterogeneity, indicating that transfer also depends on inherited population differentiation structure. Causal manipulations further suggested that the thalamus mainly affects this differentiation of cellular responses and the later learning it supports, whereas RSP may participate in routing visual cue information into the sensorimotor transformation pathway rather than constituting the core determinant of transfer success.
